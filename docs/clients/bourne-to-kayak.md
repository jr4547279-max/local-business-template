# Bourne to Kayak — concept research

## Why this business

Bourne to Kayak is an Eastbourne watersports rental business at Wish Tower Beach. Public listings describe it as a family business hiring sit-on kayaks and stand-up paddleboards, with a lifeguarded, safe and professional location. The business is already surfaced by Visit Eastbourne, but its current web presence is primarily a page inside Firstaqua rather than a focused customer journey for Bourne to Kayak.

## Verified public offering

- Sit-on kayaks
- Stand-up paddleboards (SUPs)
- Hire from Wish Tower Beach / King Edwards Parade, Eastbourne
- Booking enquiries by date and time
- Phone: 07359 668350
- Email: bournetokayak@outlook.com

## Useful proof points

- Visit Eastbourne lists Bourne to Kayak as a local kayaking attraction at Wish Tower Beach.
- Firstaqua's Bourne to Kayak page has a booking form asking for name, email, date, phone, time and message.
- A public business listing describes it as a new family business hiring sit-on kayaks and SUP boards and mentions a lifeguarded, safe and professional location.
- A July 2026 Tripadvisor review describes the business as welcoming, safe and reasonably priced.

## Website opportunity

The concept turns the existing information into a dedicated, mobile-first booking funnel:

1. Immediate hero CTA to check availability.
2. Clear separation between kayaks and SUPs.
3. Location-first presentation around Wish Tower Beach.
4. A live Eastbourne weather snapshot to help visitors decide whether to enquire.
5. Enquiry flow that captures craft, date and preferred time.
6. Explicit safety/conditions language so the weather widget is informational rather than a promise that conditions are safe.

## New capability vs the base template

`SeaConditions` fetches current Eastbourne temperature, wind, gusts and hourly precipitation probability from Open-Meteo in the browser. It includes a timestamp and explicitly states that conditions are indicative and the operator makes the final launch decision.
