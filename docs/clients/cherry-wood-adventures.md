# Cherry Wood Adventures — client concept brief

## Why this business

Cherry Wood Adventures is a strong local fit for the website factory because the offer is unusually rich but the customer journey is fragmented across many pages. The business runs outdoor activities for children and grown-ups in Sussex, with programmes ranging from ages 2–4 through home education, after-school nature sessions, wildlife/bushcraft and adult woodland events.

## Verified public offering

- Run by Sarah Wilesmith; the public site states 25 years' outdoor education experience.
- Acorn Folk: woodland playgroup for ages 2–4 at Bluebell Wood, Arlington; one-off and six-session options are published.
- Forest Folk / Forest Fridays: home-education woodland learning covering subjects including fossils, twig identification, foraging, wildlife and bushcraft.
- Nature Adventures: after-school group at Motcombe School for Year 1 and Year 2 children, including exploring, cooking, bushcraft, bug hunting, crafts and caring for nature.
- Wild Wednesdays: monthly wildlife and bushcraft studies for home-educated children around ages 10–12.
- Woodland Ways: adult sessions of just under two hours with a themed activity, making, books, tea and food.
- Seasonal events include themed woodland activities, family nature hunts and guided walks.
- Bespoke activities and events are available for adults and children.
- The public site says the business is fully insured for outdoor activities including fires, knives, climbing trees and beach activity; policies, first-aid and DBS certificates and risk assessments can be viewed on request.

## Website opportunity

The current site has excellent depth of information but behaves like an archive of individual programme pages. A parent arriving with a simple question such as “what can my 3-year-old do?” has to navigate several pages to find the right answer.

This concept keeps the warmth and woodland character but adds a single decision point: **Find your adventure**. Visitors choose the audience/age group and immediately get the most relevant real programme, its published price, a short explanation and a route into booking/contact.

## Meaningful capability added

`AdventureFinder` is a reusable interactive discovery component. It is intentionally data-driven rather than AI-dependent, so it can work without an API or database and can be updated by changing the programme data. The same pattern can be reused for holiday clubs, classes, studios, tutors, activity providers and other local businesses where the biggest conversion problem is “which service is right for me?”
