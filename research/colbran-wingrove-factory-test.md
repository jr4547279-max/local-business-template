# Factory Test — Colbran & Wingrove

## Business selected

Colbran & Wingrove is an independent residential and commercial building contractor based in the Eastbourne/Polegate area. Public business information says the company has operated since 1967 and works across East Sussex, Brighton & Hove and Tunbridge Wells.

## Why this business was selected

This is a useful factory test because construction is a strong fit for a reusable lead-generation system while still needing business-specific positioning. The business has a broad service range, a long operating history, a clear project-management proposition and a natural opportunity for a guided project-planning tool.

## Research-backed facts used

- Established in 1967.
- Residential and commercial building work.
- Extensions, alterations, loft conversions, refurbishment and repairs.
- Commercial maintenance, refurbishment, repairs, redecorating, replacement windows/roofline systems and new-build work.
- The company describes its ability to manage projects from design/surveys/analysis through construction and final finish.
- Values published by the company: Excellence, Integrity, Safe, Collaborative, Sustainable.
- Constructionline and CHAS accreditation are stated on the business website.
- Public contact details: office@colbranwingrove.co.uk and 01323 729025.
- Current contact page address: Suite G, Dittons Business Park, Dittons Road, Polegate BN26 6HY.

## Factory-specific capability added

### Project Planner

The site asks visitors to choose:

1. What they are trying to do: home improvement, commercial space, repairs/maintenance or new build.
2. Where they are in the process: idea, planning/design, ready for quotes or already underway.
3. Whether they already have an architect/design support.

The selections generate a pre-filled email brief rather than pretending to provide construction prices or a formal quote.

### Grounded Q&A demo

A lightweight question interface answers a small set of business-specific questions using researched information. It explicitly avoids inventing prices, dates or claims.

## Factory components reused

- Master business configuration
- Existing responsive layout system
- Existing enquiry form
- Existing media configuration
- Existing metadata system
- Same GitHub repository and branch/PR workflow

## What changed from the generic template

- Positioning changed from generic local service to end-to-end building partner.
- Navigation and CTA changed around projects rather than generic bookings.
- Services changed to residential/commercial construction categories.
- Added project-planning flow.
- Added grounded Q&A.
- Added business-specific process section.
- Added researched contact/service coverage.
